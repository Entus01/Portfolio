# Roadmap

## Purpose

This document describes the planned evolution of the Portfolio project.

Its purpose is to provide visibility into future initiatives, priorities, and long-term goals while maintaining alignment with the project's professional positioning and development philosophy.

Unlike the changelog, which documents completed work, the roadmap focuses on planned improvements and future direction.

---

## Objectives

- Define the future direction of the portfolio.
- Communicate planned initiatives and priorities.
- Facilitate project planning.
- Provide visibility into future improvements.
- Support long-term decision making.
- Maintain alignment between project goals and implementation efforts.

---

## When to Update this Document

Update this document when:

- New initiatives are planned.
- Project priorities change.
- Major goals are completed.
- Long-term objectives evolve.
- Significant opportunities are identified.
- Planned work becomes out of scope.

---

## When Not to Update this Document

It is not necessary to update this document for:

- Completed work already recorded in the changelog.
- Minor implementation details.
- Routine maintenance tasks.
- Small fixes.
- Temporary experiments.
- Internal refactors.

---

## Planning Principles

The roadmap should focus on initiatives rather than individual implementation tasks.

Each initiative should:

- Support the portfolio's professional positioning.
- Reinforce the Full Stack narrative.
- Improve the user experience.
- Increase maintainability.
- Contribute to long-term portfolio evolution.

The roadmap should remain flexible and may change as priorities evolve.

---

## Current Status

The project has successfully completed its architectural foundation and is transitioning from core implementation to refinement.

Current priorities include:

- Completing the Projects section.
- Refining the design system.
- Strengthening the professional narrative.
- Enhancing user experience.
- Preparing the portfolio for public release.

## Fundamental Principle

The roadmap represents the intended future direction of the project, not a commitment to implementation dates.

Priorities may evolve as the portfolio grows, new opportunities emerge, or professional goals change.

Its primary purpose is to provide strategic guidance while preserving flexibility throughout the project's evolution.
